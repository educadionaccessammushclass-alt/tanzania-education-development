About the Platform

The Tanzania National Education & Community Development Platform is an independent initiative designed to support inclusive education, evidence-based programme assessment, research, innovation and sustainable community development across Tanzania.

The platform provides a structured record of proposed, assessed, active and completed programmes. Regional records are maintained separately to support transparency, accountability and informed planning.

Administrative district and municipality lists must be populated using verified official data. The four Pemba districts are included as specified. This platform does not imply government endorsement.
